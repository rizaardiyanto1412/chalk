The garden after rain

CHAPTER 01 / the gate

A path appears.

The rain has drawn a narrow stream between the beds. Follow it to the old gate.

CHAPTER 02 / the beds

A small sign of growth.

The first shoots lean toward the open path. Leave the narrow stream clear as you pass.

CHAPTER 03 / the gate

A way back.

The gate opens onto the street. Mark this place before returning to the garden.
